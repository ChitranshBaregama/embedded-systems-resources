# Top-tier firmware roadmap

This is a personal preparation plan, not an employer assessment rubric. Starting ability is unassessed. Use a diagnostic session before selecting the first gate; do not equate prior reading with demonstrated proficiency.

## Time budget and January target

Planning baseline: 2026-09-30; the next January 1 is assumed to mean 2027-01-01. There are 93 days before that date, or 186 hours at two hours per day. Attempting all 1,500 items would require about 16.1 items/day and 7.44 minutes/item before reviews, projects or mocks. This is a stretch coverage target, not a promise of 1,500 independent coding solutions or interview readiness. Reconfirm the year if this plan is reused.

Track quick drills, medium coding and deep work separately. A skim is not an attempt. Quality, retention and gate evidence take priority over the January count. Reforecast weekly from measured task times; do not compress deep design work into token attempts to meet a number.

## Sequence and evidence

| Phase | Focus | Evidence before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | C, bit operations, array/pointer reasoning, one debugging scenario | First scored attempts with timing and hint counts |
| Foundations | Gates 1–3; short DSA alongside C and architecture | Independent code, warning-clean builds, boundary tests and explanations |
| Firmware | Gates 4–5; protocols, drivers, interrupts and RTOS | Fault injection, ownership reasoning and scheduling analysis |
| Systems | Gate 6; Linux, C++, boot, network and debug depth | Reproducible diagnosis and tradeoff discussion |
| Engineering | Gate 7 throughout the later phases | Project artifacts and staged upstream contribution evidence |
| Interview | Gate 8 after prerequisites | Three consecutive complete successful mock loops |

These phases are evidence-driven, not fixed weekly promotions. At two hours/day, use four practice days, one project day, one systems/open-source day, and one review/retention day. Adjust around existing obligations. Full mock loops span multiple daily blocks.
