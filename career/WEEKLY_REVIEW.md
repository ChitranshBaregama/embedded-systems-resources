## Definitions

Independent solve rate = zero-hint, unassisted correct attempts / all scored first attempts in the window. Report numerator and denominator and separate quick/medium/deep categories; do not combine retests with first attempts. Solve time is active minutes including debugging, excluding breaks; unsuccessful attempts retain their time. Hint count records each hint; viewing a solution is assistance even with zero conversational hints.

Compiler warnings: pass/fail/not-applicable, with command and output evidence. Debugging, test quality and explanation use the 0–4 anchors in READINESS_GATES.md. Retention is an unassisted reattempt at least 7/30 days after the original solve; a calendar reminder is not a retention pass. Projects and upstream contributions advance only with linked artifacts.
