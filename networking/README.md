Contents at a glance:

- **Part I — Foundations.** What the physical layer is and why it must exist;
  signals in time and frequency; the four impairments; the Nyquist and Shannon
  ceilings.
- **Part II — Modulation and coding.** Line coding; ASK/FSK/PSK and the
  constellation; GFSK, MSK, OQPSK, DSSS; from Eb/N0 to a sensitivity number;
  whitening, CRC, FEC and interleaving.
- **Part III — Physical media.** Copper and differential signalling; fibre,
  transceivers and dispersion; the wireless channel — path loss, multipath,
  fading, Doppler.
- **Part IV — Wireless system engineering.** Complete worked link budgets;
  interference and coexistence; FCC/ETSI duty cycle and listen-before-talk;
  antennas and RF front-ends.

Written for embedded engineers who keep hitting the PHY from above — the
person debugging why a sub-GHz link that works on the bench fails at 200 m.
