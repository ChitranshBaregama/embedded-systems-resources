# Full mock interview loop

Personal readiness rule: **three consecutive complete successful loops with no major fundamentals gap**. This is a coaching benchmark, not a prediction of a hiring outcome. All eight rounds must be scored; a partial loop cannot increment the streak.

| Round | Minutes | Required evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Coding/DSA | 40 | Learner-written code, constraints, complexity, tests |
| Deep C | 25 | Promotions, lifetime, pointer/UB reasoning |
| Embedded architecture | 25 | Reset, memory, interrupts, peripheral timing |
| Debugging | 25 | Competing hypotheses and discriminating observations |
| RTOS/concurrency | 25 | Interleavings, ownership, API constraints |
| Firmware system design | 35 | Quantified budgets, failures and recovery |
| Project deep dive | 25 | Actual artifacts, decisions, limitations and measurements |
| Behavioral communication | 20 | Truthful concrete examples and reflection |

Total interview time is 220 minutes, plus breaks and review. Split across two or three two-hour sessions; do not squeeze it into one normal day. Use unseen variants and avoid reusing a rehearsed question as independent evidence.

Score each round 0–4 using correctness, independence, reasoning and evidence: 0 no usable response; 1 major gaps; 2 partial/assisted; 3 independently meets requirements with minor recoverable errors; 4 clear, correct, tested and insightful. A successful loop requires every round at least 3 and no major gap as defined in [gates](../career/READINESS_GATES.md). Failed or incomplete rounds do not count. A failed completed loop resets the streak; an incomplete loop pauses it until finished or abandoned.

Record date range, question IDs, minutes, hints, scores per round, exact fundamentals gaps, artifact links, reviewer and remediation/retest dates. Keep hidden prompts off the learner's view until each round begins; no claim of secrecy for public committed prompts.
